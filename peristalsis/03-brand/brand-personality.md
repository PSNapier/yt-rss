# Brand Personality — Peristalsis

**Phase:** 5 — Brand
**Project:** peristalsis
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Confidence:** Medium ([Opinion])

---

## Archetype: **The Creator** (primary), with a **Sage** streak

- **Creator** — the brand is about *making* (curating your own algorithm, building groups, shaping a graph). It hands users tools and taste, and celebrates what they build. Fits the graph-as-dopamine-toy and shareable-packs pillars.
- **Sage** (secondary) — knowing, honest, demystifies the algorithm ("here's what YouTube's actually doing to your feed"). Appeals to the tech-literate, distrust-of-platform audience.
- **Explicitly not the Rebel/Outlaw** — anti-algorithm sentiment is real, but a "burn it down / stick it to YouTube" tone would clash with the companion positioning and heighten perceived ToS hostility. Channel rebellion into *empowerment* (Creator), not *destruction*.

## Emotional Attributes
- **Relief** — "finally, I see everything again."
- **Control/agency** — "I decide, not the algorithm."
- **Quiet satisfaction** — the calm competence of a well-organized system (the graph, inbox-zero feed).
- **Trust** — independent, portable, no tracking; won't vanish with your data.

## How Peristalsis should feel vs. competitors
| Competitor | Their feel | Peristalsis contrast |
|---|---|---|
| PocketTube / Unhook (extensions) | Utilitarian, bolted-on, fragile | Deliberate, whole, reliable — a place, not a patch |
| Inoreader / NewsBlur (RSS) | Dense, businesslike, "article-y" | YouTube-native, visual, playful |
| Feedvault | Aggressive, priced-at-you, cold | Generous, honest, human (solo maker who replies) |
| YouTube itself | Slick but manipulative, opaque | Transparent, calm, on-your-side |

## Visual Direction (adjectives + references, not final design)
- **Adjectives:** calm, spacious, tactile, confident, subtly playful, "quiet dark mode by default."
- **The graph is the hero visual** — an Obsidian-style constellation of channels is inherently beautiful and shareable; lean on it for identity, marketing embeds, OG images.
- **References (directional):** Obsidian's graph view (structure + satisfaction), Readwise Reader (calm density done right), Linear (crisp, confident, no fluff), Fathom Analytics (privacy-first, honest indie tone).
- **Anti-references:** cluttered dashboards, gamified streak-badges everywhere, neon "hustle SaaS" gradients, anything that feels engagement-maximizing.
- **Palette instinct [Opinion]:** dark, low-glare base with a single warm accent — the feed is used daily/at night; calm > loud. (Validate against YouTube branding-guideline constraints before finalizing marks — see `raw/regulatory.md` §1.4.)

## Naming note
"Peristalsis" (the gut's involuntary push that moves things through) is an unusual, memorable, slightly nerdy name that fits the Sage/Creator + tech-literate audience. It reinforces the "push feed" (WebSub push) mechanic. **Risk:** hard to spell/say, no keyword-SEO value. [Opinion] Keep as the brand name but ensure the CWS extension + SEO surfaces use descriptive titles ("YouTube Subscriptions Feed — chronological, grouped") for discovery (see `go-to-market.md`).
