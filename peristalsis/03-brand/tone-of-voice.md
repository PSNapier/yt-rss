# Tone of Voice — Peristalsis

**Phase:** 5 — Brand
**Project:** peristalsis
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Confidence:** Medium ([Opinion], grounded in customer language map)

---

## Personality Traits

1. **Direct** — plain language, no marketing fog. The audience (tech-literate power users) distrusts hype.
2. **Knowing** — talks like someone who *also* fights the YouTube feed daily; shared-frustration insider tone.
3. **Calm/confident** — reassuring, not shouty. We fix an annoyance; we don't manufacture urgency.
4. **Playful (in moments)** — the graph and curation are fun; a dry wit is allowed, never at the expense of clarity.

## Voice Principles (we are / we are not)

- **We are** concrete ("see every upload from channels you chose") — **not** vague ("optimize your viewing experience").
- **We are** honest about limits ("YouTube can change things; here's how we stay resilient") — **not** overpromising ("never breaks, guaranteed").
- **We are** respectful of intelligence (assume they know what RSS is) — **not** condescending or over-explaining.
- **We are** anti-algorithm in framing, **pro-creator** in substance — **not** anti-YouTube (we're a companion).
- **We are** quietly witty — **not** meme-chasing or trying-too-hard.

## Writing Samples

**Homepage headline:**
> **Curate your own algorithm.**
> Every video from the channels you chose — grouped, complete, on every device. You keep watching on YouTube.

**Subhead:**
> YouTube decides what you see. Peristalsis hands that back to you.

**Empty state (no subscriptions yet):**
> Nothing here yet. Import your subscriptions (or drop in an OPML file) and we'll pull in everything — nothing hidden.

**Error message (feed fetch failed):**
> Couldn't reach one of your channels' feeds just now. We'll retry automatically — nothing's lost. (This is usually YouTube, not you.)

**Social post (feed degradation news):**
> YouTube just killed the last chronological-feed workaround. Again. Your subscriptions, decided for you. There's another way → [link]

**Upgrade prompt (Premium):**
> You've built a serious setup. Sync it across your devices, save filter presets, and publish your curated packs — from ~$5/mo. No ads. No tracking. Funded by you.

**Customer email (welcome):**
> Hey — thanks for trying Peristalsis. One thing worth doing first: open the channel graph. It's oddly satisfying to see your whole media diet laid out. Reply anytime; a human (me) reads these.

## Vocabulary Guide

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "curate your own algorithm" | "AI-powered," "smart feed" |
| "every video / nothing hidden / complete" | "never miss out" (FOMO framing) |
| "channels you chose" | "content," "creators you might like" |
| "companion / you keep watching on YouTube" | "replacement," "alternative front-end" |
| "your feed, your lists, yours to export" | "our platform," "lock-in" |
| "calm," "in control," "on your terms" | "addictive," "can't stop watching" |
| "independent, user-funded, no tracking" | "monetize," "engagement" |
