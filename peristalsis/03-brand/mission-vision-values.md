## Mission (why we exist — pick/remix)

**Option A (recommended):** *"Give people back control of what they watch — curate your own algorithm, on your terms."*

**Option B:** *"Make following the channels you love simple, complete, and calm — never at the mercy of an algorithm."*

**Option C:** *"Turn your subscriptions into a feed you own, organize, and share — instead of one that's decided for you."*

> Recommended: **A** — shortest, present-tense, action-oriented, and carries the core "curate your own algorithm" hook.

## Vision (the world we're building toward)

**Option A (recommended):** *"A web where you decide what surfaces — where following someone means you actually see them, and your media diet is yours to shape and share."*

**Option B:** *"A world where 'the algorithm' is something you build, not something done to you."*

> Recommended: **B** as a tagline-length rallying cry; **A** as the fuller statement. Both credible — they describe control, not a moonshot.

## Values (decision-guiding, not platitudes)

1. **You own your feed.** Portability and export are non-negotiable. Every feature must survive us disappearing (OPML/channel-ID import, one-click export). *In practice:* we never build a lock-in we couldn't let a user walk away from.
2. **Completeness over cleverness.** Never silently hide a video. When in doubt, show it and let the user filter. *In practice:* no "smart" ranking that drops content without the user asking.
3. **Calm, not capture.** We're a tool, not a slot machine. Success = the user watches what they chose and leaves satisfied — not maximized watch-time. *In practice:* no engagement-bait, no infinite autoplay, no dark patterns.
4. **Companion, not parasite.** We drive traffic *to* YouTube and creators; we never strip ads or proxy video. *In practice:* playback stays on YouTube, always.
5. **Independent and user-funded.** No ads, no tracking, no selling behavioral data — funded by the people who use it. *In practice:* the business model can never require monetizing user attention or data.

> **Note on honesty:** these values are also strategic necessities — "you own your feed" and "companion, not parasite" are the ToS-risk mitigations (see `01-discovery/market-analysis.md`, `raw/regulatory.md`). Brand and survival align here, which makes them credible rather than decorative.
