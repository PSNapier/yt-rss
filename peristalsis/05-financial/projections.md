## Three Scenarios (Year-1 ARR)

| | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free signups (M12 cum.) | ~6,000 | ~10,500 | ~18,000 |
| Free→paid conversion | 1.2% | 2.0% | 3.0% |
| Blended ARPU | $5.50 | $7.25 | $9.50 |
| Monthly churn | 10% | 8% | 6% |
| Payers (EoY) | ~55 | ~150 | ~380 |
| **Year-1 ARR** | **~$4K** | **~$13K** | **~$43K** |
| Interpretation | "expensive hobby" | indie side income | strong indie / near quit-job |

### Key Assumptions
- **Conservative:** WTP is as weak as proxies suggest (donation culture wins); free tier cannibalizes; churn brutal.
- **Base:** conversion at the low end of prosumer norm; modest supporter mix (15%); annual plans blunt churn to 8%.
- **Optimistic:** graph + sharing loop drives virality; supporter mix rises to ~25%; strong annual adoption; retention improves toward 6%.

## Cash-Flow Timeline (base)
- Fixed costs ~$55–190/mo from day one; one-time ~$500–4K (mostly legal, pre-paid-launch).
- **Cash-positive on operations by ~M2–M3** (break-even ~9–30 payers; see `cost-structure.md`).
- Cumulative Y1 revenue (base) ~$4–6K collected; comfortably covers ~$1–3K Y1 cash costs. **The business does not need external funding.**

## Funding Needs & Runway
- **Funding needed: $0 external.** This is a bootstrapped, cash-positive-fast side project. Out-of-pocket downside is capped at low hundreds/mo + one-time legal.
- **The scarce resource is founder time, not money.** The honest "runway" question: *how many months of solo hours is the founder willing to spend before the numbers justify continuing?* Recommend a **time-box**: if by ~M6 post-launch the product isn't clearing the base-case trajectory (≥~60 payers / ≥$400 MRR) or showing improving retention, treat it as a lifestyle tool, not a growing business (ties to Phase 8 kill-criteria).

## Model Viability Judgment (feeds final dashboard)
**RISKY (but low-downside).** The cost side is a genuine strength (near-zero marginal cost, 70–80% margin, funding-free). The revenue side is entirely hostage to the unvalidated WTP assumption. Because downside is capped (no capital at risk, founder builds it for himself anyway) and upside is a real indie income, the risk profile is *asymmetric and acceptable* — provided the founder time-boxes and validates WTP before over-investing hours.

## Benchmark Reconciliation
- ARPU $5–9.50 vs. prosumer healthy $10–50 → **below range** (flagged; supporter tier is the lever).
- Conversion 1.2–3% vs. 2–5% → **low end** (donation-culture drag, justified).
- Gross margin 70–80% → **in range** [Data].
- Churn 6–10% vs. 3–7% → **above range** (consumer reality; annual mix is the mitigation).

---

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- Revenue realism hinges entirely on WTP; conservative case ($4K ARR) is "expensive hobby" territory — plausible given the evidence.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Above-benchmark churn + below-benchmark ARPU compound to cap LTV; the model only works if annual plans + supporter tier both land.
- Optimistic case leans on the sharing/graph virality that is itself unproven (two-sided cold-start).
